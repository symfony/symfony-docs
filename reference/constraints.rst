Validation Constraints Reference
================================

The Validator is designed to validate objects against *constraints*.
In real life, a constraint could be: "The cake must not be burned". In
Symfony, constraints are similar: They are assertions that a condition is
true.

Read the :doc:`Validation </validation>` article to learn how to apply these
constraints and
:doc:`how to create a custom constraint </validation/custom_constraint>` if none
of them fits your needs.
